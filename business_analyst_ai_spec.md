# AI Business Analyst Behaviour Specification

How the system answers the questions an owner actually asks. Every behaviour below is the live
behaviour, recorded in `business_question_matrix.md`, which is generated by running each question
through the real system.

---

## 1. The answer contract, in owner terms

Every answer carries — whether or not the owner asks for them:

```
the figure (or why there isn't one) · what it means · how it was calculated ·
what evidence backs it · whether it was validated · what caveats attach ·
what conflicts exist · what would change the answer
```

Levels 2–4 are one click away, never removed.

---

## 2. Behaviour by question

### "How is the business doing?"
→ **Management briefing.** All 7 sections. Profit, receivables and occupancy appear as competing
definitions, not as figures. Materiality of every change is explicitly undefined.
*Lenses: Management Reporting + Business + Risk/DQ.*

### "What changed?" · "Compare this month with last month."
→ **Period-change list.** Direction and size for each comparable metric. **Incomplete periods are
excluded and named** — the export snapshot is mid-month, and comparing a partial month against a
complete one manufactured a spurious "revenue fell 99.86%" during development. That is an
artifact of the export cut-off, not a business event.

### "Why did profit fall?"
→ **BLOCK + driver decomposition, per definition.** Profit has three incompatible definitions, so
no single "profit fell by X" statement is made. Each definition is decomposed separately over its
*documented* dependency edges and the decompositions are never merged.
*Lenses: Financial + Data Scientist + Business + Risk/DQ.*

### "Why are collections down?" · "Why are dues increasing?" · "Why is occupancy low?"
→ **Driver analysis.** Documented edges only. Every driver statement is an OBSERVATION or a
hedged HYPOTHESIS, never a cause:

> "This is a correlation over a documented dependency edge, not a demonstrated cause. The
> exported evidence contains no experiment or control that could establish causation."

### "Which area needs attention?" · "What should I fix first?" · "What should I do today?"
→ **Decision list.** Attention items (owner decisions) then recommendations, each with evidence,
impact, confidence, action, and what would change it.

### "What are the biggest risks?" · "Where am I losing money?"
→ **Ranked composite.** Each contributing metric keeps its own metric_id and trust label,
presented as a labelled list — never merged into one risk score, because no such metric exists.

### "Which tenants need attention?"
→ **Risk scan at tenant grain.** Touches the AR family (SHOW_BOTH/BLOCK) and aging (DISCLOSE), so
the dues component shows all four definitions.

### "Are expenses increasing?"
→ **Period comparison** on `M.EXP.001`, materiality undefined.

### "What caused this change?" (with no prior context)
→ **Clarification.** "This change" has no referent. Asking which measure is correct; reporting
that no metric exists would be false.

### "Show me the important business insights." · "Give me the management report." · "What should I tell the owner?"
→ **Briefing.**

### "Is there anything unusual?"
→ **Cannot answer, and says why.** Anomaly detection requires a statistical method that
`analytics_execution_spec.md` §2.5 explicitly leaves open. No threshold is invented.

### "Can I trust this number?"
→ **Trust posture.** All 49 metrics bucketed by verdict, with what each level means in business
terms.

## 3. The reasoning ladder

Every answer climbs only as far as its intent's ceiling
(`business_reasoning_spec.md` §3):

| Intent | Ceiling |
|---|---|
| Lookup | CALCULATION |
| Trend / Comparison | OBSERVATION |
| Anomaly | OBSERVATION |
| Driver | INFERENCE, HYPOTHESIS where evidence runs out |
| Risk scan | INFERENCE |
| Recommendation | RECOMMENDATION |

**One documented exception:** a SHOW_BOTH/BLOCK metric reaches INFERENCE even on a bare lookup,
because `ai_trust_policy.md` requires the conflict disclosure regardless of intent. The raise is
recorded (`ceiling_raised_for_disclosure`), capped at INFERENCE, and never licenses a
recommendation the owner did not ask for.

A Lookup answered with an unsolicited RECOMMENDATION violates the specification just as surely as
a Recommendation answered with a bare number.

---

## 4. Conversation

Follow-ups inherit **additively only**. A field the owner supplied is never overwritten:

```
"How much revenue did we make last month?"  →  "Why was it lower?"
   inherits: concept, metric, period.  becomes: a driver question.

"Show revenue for July."  →  "What about June?"
   inherits: metric.  does NOT inherit: the period — June was stated explicitly.
```

A **correction** ("no, I meant occupancy") replaces context rather than extending it: carrying
the prior state forward would preserve the very error being corrected.

A **definition selection** is honoured only after the owner has been shown the alternatives, and
only when unambiguous. "Use the live occupancy definition" matches three of five labels, so the
system asks which — picking one would be the silent narrowing the trust policy forbids; ignoring
the instruction would leave it unacknowledged.

---

## 5. What the AI Business Analyst never does

| Never | Enforced by |
|---|---|
| State a figure for a BLOCK metric | Gate; verbalization guard |
| Choose among SHOW_BOTH definitions | Gate; explicit-selection requirement |
| Assert a cause | Root-cause guard; hedge-language requirement |
| Invent a materiality or anomaly threshold | No threshold exists; reported as undefined |
| Estimate a NOT_DETERMINABLE value | Must state exactly "Not determinable from exported evidence." |
| Surface PII | Guard over 27 excluded columns |
| Let the LLM's number stand | Every figure must already exist in the deterministic skeleton |
